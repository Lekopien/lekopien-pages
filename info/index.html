About the studio

We build websites, and fix the ones that broke

Lekopien is a web studio for small businesses and creators. We design and build fast, mobile-first sites, and we troubleshoot slow, broken or hacked ones.

Start a projectSee our work

Services

New websites

Landing pages, business sites and online stores, designed for phones first.

Fixes and rescue

Broken pages, errors after updates, slow loading, hacked sites and lost access.

Speed and SEO

Performance audits, Core Web Vitals, metadata, sitemaps and Search Console.

Care plans

Updates, backups, uptime monitoring and small changes every month.

How we work

  1. Chat: tell us about your business or the problem, through the chat or contact form on lekopien.com.
  2. Plan and quote: we send a clear scope, timeline and price before any work starts.
  3. Build: you see progress on a private preview link and give feedback.
  4. Launch: we connect your domain, set up HTTPS and hand over everything you need.
