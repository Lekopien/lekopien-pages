Dietetics & Clinical Nutrition

Clinical nutrition you and your doctor can trust

My practice is separate from general fitness coaching. Every plan starts from a clinical assessment and follows evidence-based guidelines, and I welcome referrals from doctors, clinics and physiotherapists.

Credentials & compliance

  • Degree,
  • Regulatory bodyKenya Nutritionists and Dieticians Institute (KNDI)
  • KNDI registration number
  • Data protectionClient records handled under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Read the privacy policy.
For referring clinicians: send a short referral note with diagnosis, current medication and recent labs to the patient, or ask the patient to share it on WhatsApp before their first session. I send a written summary of the nutrition plan back on request, with the patient’s consent.

Clinical specialities

Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT)

  • Type 2 diabetes and prediabetes
  • Hypertension (DASH-style eating)
  • Cholesterol and cardiovascular health
  • Fatty liver and metabolic syndrome

Metabolic & Weight Management

  • Sustainable fat loss without crash diets
  • Body composition and muscle gain
  • PCOS-friendly eating patterns
  • Repairing an on-off dieting history

Sports Nutrition & Performance

  • Fuelling around training and matches
  • Hydration and electrolytes
  • Protein needs and supplements that are worth it
  • Recovery, sleep and energy availability

The process

1

Comprehensive Assessment

Clinical markers (blood sugar, HbA1c, lipids, blood pressure), body composition, medications, dietary history and lifestyle.

2

Personalised Programming

A written plan with meal structures, portions using local foods, and a shopping list that fits your budget and schedule.

3

Continuous Support

Scheduled follow-ups to review progress, new lab results and how the plan feels in real life, then adjust.
