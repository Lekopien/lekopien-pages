About Sarran Mahonye Otieno

Clinical Dietitian & Nutritionist

Welcome! I am Sarran, a registered Clinical Dietitian and Nutritionist dedicated to helping you cut through the internet noise and build a sustainable, science-backed relationship with food.

My approach to nutrition is rooted strictly in clinical science, but tailored for real life. Whether you are managing a chronic metabolic condition, trying to optimise your body composition, or looking to fuel intense athletic performance, I design evidence-based medical nutrition therapies that respect your lifestyle, culture, and personal goals.

I believe that healthy eating shouldn’t feel like a life sentence. Having spent years immersed in Nairobi’s active fitness, gym, and creative culture, I understand firsthand the challenges of balancing a busy schedule with optimal physical health. I don’t believe in restrictive fads or one-size-fits-all meal plans; I build dynamic, data-driven nutritional strategies that power your daily life.

When I am not analysing clinical biomarkers or designing custom client protocols, you can usually find me lifting at the gym, collaborating with local fitness creators, sitting at a chess arbiter’s table, or losing track of time on the dancefloor. Yes, I am a massive oontz head at heart, fuelled by good coffee and deep electronic beats.

Let’s work together to optimise your health, from the inside out.
