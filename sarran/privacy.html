Privacy Policy & Telehealth Consent

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the private dietetics practice of Sarran Mahonye Otieno (“we”, “our”, “the Practice”) collects, uses, protects and discloses your personal and clinical information when you use our online telehealth services and this website. It is written to meet the requirements of Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information we collect

To provide accurate Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) and custom meal planning, we collect the following during your online onboarding and consultation:

  • Identity and contact data: full name, date of birth, gender, email address and phone number.
  • Clinical and health data: medical history, current diagnoses, medication lists, recent blood work and lab reports, dietary logs, body measurements (weight, height, body fat percentage) and fitness goals.
  • Payment data: M-Pesa transaction references and the name and number shown on the payment. We never ask for or store card numbers or your M-Pesa PIN.

This website itself does not store anything you type. The booking form only prepares a WhatsApp message on your own device; nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Please do not put medical details in that first message.

2. How we use your information

Your data is used only for professional clinical purposes:

  • Assessing your nutritional status and designing your clinical diet plan.
  • Running your virtual consultations.
  • Sending follow-up plans, educational resources and payment confirmations.
  • Keeping clinical records to the standard expected of nutrition professionals registered with the Kenya Nutritionists and Dieticians Institute (KNDI).

Health data is sensitive personal data under the Act. We process it on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you book, and to provide you with health care.

3. Data protection, security and telehealth consent

  • Confidentiality: your clinical information is treated as confidential health information. It is never sold, rented or shared with marketers.
  • Storage: intake forms, plans and lab results are kept in password-protected accounts with two-step verification, and access is limited to Sarran.
  • Sharing with your care team: we only share information with your doctor or another health professional if you ask us to, or where the law requires it.
  • Telehealth risk acknowledgement: online consultations rely on internet networks and devices. We use end-to-end encrypted messaging (WhatsApp) and secure video platforms, but no digital transmission is completely risk-free. By booking an online consultation you consent to receiving nutritional care remotely and acknowledge these risks.
  • Limits of telehealth: online nutrition care does not replace emergency or in-person medical care. If you have a medical emergency, contact your doctor or go to the nearest hospital.

4. Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted platforms to run the practice:

  • Booking and messaging: WhatsApp (Meta).
  • Video consultations: Google Meet or Zoom.
  • Payments: M-Pesa (Safaricom).
  • Website hosting: Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

These providers have their own privacy policies covering how they handle your basic contact and transaction information.

5. How long we keep your data

Clinical records are kept for as long as professional and legal record-keeping rules require, after which they are securely deleted. Contact details used only for booking are deleted when no longer needed.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used, and access your client file;
  • ask for incorrect or incomplete data to be corrected;
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect care already given);
  • ask for your personal contact data to be deleted, subject to medical record retention requirements;
  • complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.

Telehealth consent
