Payment

Paying for your project

Simple, transparent payments. You always get a written quote first, and an invoice and receipt for every payment.

How it works

1. Quote

We agree the scope, timeline and total price in writing.

2. Deposit

A deposit reserves your slot and starts the work.

3. Build and review

You review the preview and request changes.

4. Balance and launch

Pay the balance and we go live and hand over access.

Payment methods

M-Pesa

Bank transfer

All payments should be made in Kenyan Shillings (KES). Always use your invoice number as the payment reference. Never send money to any number or account that isn't on your invoice or this page.

Questions

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, larger projects can be split into milestones. Ask when you request your quote.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes, every payment is confirmed with a receipt by email.

What about card payments?

Card payments aren't available yet. M-Pesa and bank transfer are.
