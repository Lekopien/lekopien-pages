Launch checklist

    Guides

    Domains and DNS

    Buy the domain in your own name and keep the login. Point it with an A or CNAME record; changes can take up to a day to spread. Turn on auto-renew.

    Speed

    Compress images (WebP or AVIF), lazy-load anything below the fold, limit third-party scripts, and test with PageSpeed Insights on mobile.

    SEO basics

    One clear title and description per page, real headings, descriptive image alt text, a sitemap, and your business on Google Business Profile.

    Security

    HTTPS everywhere, unique passwords with two-factor login, updates applied promptly, and backups you've actually tested restoring.

    When your site breaks

    Note what changed last, take screenshots of errors, check if the domain or hosting expired, then contact us with the URL.

    Writing content

    Say who you help and how in the first line. Add prices or "from" prices, real photos, reviews, and one clear call to action per page.
